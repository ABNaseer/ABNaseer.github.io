About

Software engineer by day, vibecoder by night. My time splits between the two.

I work full time at Web7Labs, after a stretch of freelance work for a few startups. It's a small team, so I cover most of the roles myself: engineering, product, QA, design, whatever a given week needs. We build on Next.js and meet with the owner every week, and half the job is reining in scope creep since requirements shift constantly. Most days come down to sipping tea and watching Claude Code. I graduated in the Batch of '26, closing it out with 1st position in my FYP.

Football takes up a good share of my free time. I play as much as my body allows, and I've followed Bayern Munich since 2014, through the trophies and the robberies. Javi Martinez was my favorite back then. Olise has that spot now.

A playlist for every mood runs behind all of this. You can see what I'm playing on Last.fm.

Most weekends end in a lobby with the guys, and I always land back on LoL, Valo, and Minecraft. FM comes out when I'd rather drown in a weekend of spreadsheets than go outside.
